One engine, no site configuration: it was given only each shop's URL. Across 6 shop variants (3 structurally different shops, each dark and honest) it found 21 of 21 seeded dark patterns covering all 13 CCPA categories, with 0 false alarms. It also audited 4 real public test shops read-only.

How it works

1 · DiscoverReads the home page, visits the pages that matter, and picks the most promoted product and any paid plan.
2 · WalkA careful and a hurried simulated user pursue the same plain-English goal. Both stop before payment.
3 · ProbeFresh sessions over time, buying more than the "only N left", refusing cookies, watching for downloads.
4 · JudgeEach CCPA category is a comparison: choice vs basket, first price vs final, join vs cancel effort.

The local model proposes (what a control does, where a price is, what a claim says); fixed code decides. Amounts must appear verbatim on the page, quotes must be found on the page, and control states are read from the page itself.
